Spanish For Night Owls

Spanish For Night Owls is less a lesson than a seduction. It lives in that strange, beautiful hour when the city softens, the lights blur, and everything starts to feel a little more cinematic.

Built on warm Spanish guitar, smoked-out beats, late-night jazz textures, and vocals that drift between intimacy and mystery, the record feels like a whispered conversation on a rooftop at 1:37 a.m. There’s romance here, but it’s not sweet. It’s moodier than that. More knowing. More electric. Like neon on wet pavement, radio static in the distance, and a language you feel before you fully understand it.

This is music for insomniacs, lovers, smokers, wanderers, and anyone who’s ever learned more from the night than they did during the day.

"Spanish For Night Owls" - doesn’t ask for perfect fluency.

It asks you to Stay Awake.
