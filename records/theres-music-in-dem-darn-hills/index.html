There’s Music In Dem Darn Hills

Historic field recordings collide with contemporary production—past and present stitched together with a cold, steady hand. This isn’t nostalgia. It’s excavation: degraded voices, distant rooms, damaged tape, and fragments that were never meant to outlive the moment.

Each track lands like evidence—part document, part séance—where modern drums act as a heartbeat beneath something older that won’t stay quiet. You can hear the air in the room, the warble of the reel, the ghost of a sentence cut off mid-thought. Memory becomes texture. Silence becomes threat. The closer you listen, the more you listen, the recording are listening back.

Headphones recommended. The past isn’t resonating... It’s waking up.
