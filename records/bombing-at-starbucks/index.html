Bombing At Starbucks

A:.A:.

Released in 2022, Bombing At Starbucks by A:.A:. (AKA ChemTrails, AKA Gamecat) is a three-track ambient release that drifts through globalization, corporate sameness, paranoia, and the weird psychic fog of modern life. Built from pre-9/11 field recordings and samples captured in Istanbul and other parts of Eastern Europe, the album feels less like a straightforward document and more like a haunted transmission—nostalgic, dislocated, and quietly unnerving.

There are obvious shades of Twin Peaks here, especially in “Black Lodge” and the title track, where eerie synths, degraded textures, and negative space create that Lynchian sense of dread where something feels wrong, even if you can’t fully name it. But what gives the record more weight is that it’s not just chasing mood. “No Martians” digs into the overlap between corporate mythology, conspiracy culture, and the strange fictions people start building when reality itself begins to feel overproduced.

What makes Bombing At Starbucks land is that it refuses to be just background music. It works more like a piece of sonic criticism—using atmosphere, ambiguity, and dislocation to reflect a world where branding, fear, nostalgia, and ideology have all started bleeding together. It’s eerie, thoughtful, and a little ugly in the right ways—like cultural decay turned into ambience.
