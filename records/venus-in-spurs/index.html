Venus In Spurs

ButterFly Incorporated

From the vibrant streets of San Francisco comes Venus In Spurs, an alt-rock band that merges the gritty edge of the Velvet Underground with the experimental flair of Can. Led by the visionary Scott Burgess, the band's sound is a kaleidoscope of raw emotion and innovative rhythms. Bassist Raul Vargas is the heartbeat of their music, crafting rich, catchy bass tones that drive the band's punchy and distinctive groove. Drummer Greg Piper, a renowned figure in the Bay Area music scene, breaks tradition with his cutting-edge electronic drum set, infusing a modern twist into their retro-inspired soundscapes. Keyboardist Steven Hubbard adds a layer of nostalgic brilliance with his keys, often reminiscent of Ray Manzarek, using vintage 1970s keyboards but favoring the versatility of his Nord Stage. Together, Venus In Spurs create a sonic experience that is both timeless and ahead of its time.
